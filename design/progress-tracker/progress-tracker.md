A subtask is valid only if an external observer can decide whether it is complete from evidence.

Progress is computed over leaf subtasks.

A parent task with children is organizational and does not directly count.

Completed leaves contribute their weight to the numerator.

Active leaves contribute their weight to the denominator.

Invalidated/deleted leaves remain in history but do not count in the active denominator.

Progress can decrease when the denominator expands or completed work is reopened.

Example:

Step 0:
  4 active subtasks, 0 complete
  progress = 0 / 4 = 0.00

Step 5:
  4 active subtasks, 2 complete
  progress = 2 / 4 = 0.50

Step 8:
  tests reveal 4 more required subtasks
  progress = 2 / 8 = 0.25

Step 9:
  one completed subtask was wrong and is reopened
  progress = 1 / 8 = 0.125
